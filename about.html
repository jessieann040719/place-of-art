Our Story

About Us

Welcome to Place of Art Tattoo Shop & Gallery.

Place of Art grew from a lifelong connection to art and the belief that tattooing is more than creating an image on skin. Every piece can carry a story, memory, idea or part of the person who wears it.

Where It Began

Founder and tattoo artist Jessie-Ann Odell began tattooing professionally in 2016. That same year, she opened her first tattoo studio, Saint Ink, in Heilsbronn, Germany. Those early years became an important foundation for her development as both an artist and a business owner. Running an independent studio meant learning how to lead, work closely with people and their stories, build trust with clients, and continuously develop new artistic and technical skills.

A New Chapter

In 2021, Jessie-Ann and her family made the decision to move from Germany to the United States with her then two-year-old son. Closing Saint Ink marked the end of her first chapter as a studio owner, but not the end of the vision behind her work.

After arriving in the United States, Jessie-Ann completed the necessary licensing and requirements and began building again from the ground up. With determination, hard work and years of experience behind her, she opened Place of Art — a new studio where she could bring together everything she had learned while allowing her creativity and vision to grow in a new direction.

Place of Art Today

The studio quickly found a place within the local community. Over time, Place of Art grew from one artist's vision into a team of talented artists, each bringing their own specialties, personality and artistic perspective. Today, Place of Art Tattoo Shop & Gallery is a creative space centered around individuality, craftsmanship, continuous growth and the people behind every piece of art we create.
