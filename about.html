Welcome to Place of Art Tattoo Shop & Gallery

Place of Art grew from a lifelong connection to art and the belief that tattooing is about far more than placing an image on skin. Every tattoo can carry a memory, an idea, a story, or a part of the person who wears it.

Where the Journey Began

Founder and tattoo artist Jessie-Ann Odell began tattooing professionally in 2016 after being deeply connected to art and creativity for as long as she can remember. That same year, she opened her first tattoo studio, Saint Ink, in Heilsbronn, Germany.

Those early years became the foundation for everything that followed. Running an independent studio allowed Jessie-Ann to grow not only as a tattoo artist, but also as a business owner and leader. She gained experience working closely with people and the personal stories behind their tattoos, managing the responsibilities of self-employment, working in a leadership role, and continuously developing her artistic and technical skills.

A New Chapter in the United States

In 2021, Jessie-Ann and her family made the decision to leave Germany and begin a new chapter in the United States. She moved with her family and her son, who was two years old at the time. Closing Saint Ink marked the end of her first chapter as a studio owner, but it did not end the vision behind her work.

After arriving in the United States, Jessie-Ann began rebuilding from the ground up. She completed the necessary licensing and requirements and, through determination, hard work and years of experience, opened a new tattoo studio where she could bring together everything she had learned while giving her creativity room to evolve.

The Beginning of Place of Art

That new chapter became Place of Art Tattoo Shop & Gallery. The studio was created as more than a place to get tattooed. It was built as a creative space where tattooing, art, individuality and personal expression could exist together.

Jessie-Ann was warmly welcomed into the local community, built new connections and continued developing the studio around both artistic quality and the experience of the people who walk through its doors.

Place of Art Today

What began as one artist's vision has grown into a team of talented artists, each bringing their own specialties, personality and artistic perspective. Today, Place of Art represents craftsmanship, continuous growth, creativity and the belief that there is always more to learn. Most importantly, it remains centered around the people, stories and ideas behind every piece of art we create.
