Welcome to Place of Art

More than a tattoo studio.

Place of Art is a tattoo shop and gallery focused on custom artwork, professional tattooing and an individual experience for every client.

Our artists specialize in a range of styles including Fine Line, Realism, Micro Realism, Traditional, Blackwork, Cyberpunk and ornamental tattooing.
