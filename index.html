Welcome to Place of Art

Art that becomes part of you.

Place of Art is a tattoo shop and gallery built around creativity, craftsmanship and individuality.

Our artists work across a variety of styles including Fine Line, Realism, Micro Realism, Traditional, Blackwork, Cyberpunk and ornamental tattooing.

Whether you're looking for a delicate Fine Line piece or a large custom project, our goal is to create artwork designed specifically for you.
