Jessie-Ann Odell

Realism · Fine Line · Micro Realism · 10+ Years Experience

Biography

Jessie-Ann has been tattooing professionally since 2016 and has been deeply connected to art for as long as she can remember. Her work focuses on Realism, Fine Line and Micro Realism, with an emphasis on detail, composition and creating pieces that feel personal to the client.
