General Aftercare

Keep your tattoo clean, avoid unnecessary touching, follow your artist's bandage instructions, and avoid soaking the tattoo while it heals. Do not pick or scratch peeling skin. Protect healing tattoos from unnecessary sun exposure and use only products your artist recommends for your situation.
