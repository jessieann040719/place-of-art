Studio Rules

Studio Policy

Please review these policies before submitting an appointment request.

Important: This is a professionally structured studio-policy template, not legal advice. Before relying on it as your final legal document, have the final wording and your digital consent workflow reviewed for your Oklahoma business.

Age & Identification

Place of Art provides tattoo services to clients age 18 and older only. A valid government-issued photo ID showing the client's identity and date of birth is required before tattooing. Tattoo procedures will not be performed on a person under 18 years of age.

Ability to Consent

Clients must be able to provide informed consent. Place of Art will not tattoo a person who appears impaired by alcohol or controlled substances or otherwise unable to understand the procedure, risks and aftercare instructions.

Appointment Requests & Confirmation

Submitting an online booking form creates an appointment request only. Selecting an available date and time does not guarantee the appointment. A request becomes confirmed only after Place of Art or the selected artist accepts it. The studio may propose a different artist, date, time or appointment length when necessary.

Deposits

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required for most appointments. Appointments priced at $500 or more require a $100 non-refundable deposit. Deposits are credited toward the tattoo price when the appointment is completed as scheduled. Large-scale, sleeve and other custom projects may display the total tattoo price as Custom / TBD until the artist reviews the project.

Rescheduling, Cancellations & No-Shows

Please provide at least 48 hours notice when requesting to reschedule. Deposits are non-refundable. Late cancellations, failure to appear, repeated rescheduling or insufficient notice may result in forfeiture of the deposit and may require a new deposit before another appointment is scheduled.

Late Arrivals

Arriving late may reduce the tattooing time available or require the appointment to be rescheduled when the delay would affect another client's appointment or the artist's schedule.

Design Changes

Reference images are used for direction and inspiration. The artist may adapt scale, composition, placement or details so the tattoo works appropriately for the body and the artist's style. Significant changes to the requested design, size, quantity or placement may change the price and appointment length and may require rescheduling.

Pricing

Website prices are starting or category prices unless a fixed price is specifically stated. Final pricing may vary based on size, placement, detail, complexity, color, shading and time required. Custom sleeves, large-scale work and some highly detailed projects are priced individually.

Health, Skin Condition & Safety

Clients may be asked to complete health-history and release/consent documentation before the procedure. The studio may postpone or refuse tattooing when skin is visibly irritated, injured or infected, when a client cannot provide valid consent, or when proceeding would conflict with applicable health and safety requirements.

Client Records & Written Consent

Required client, identification, procedure and consent records are treated as confidential studio records. Online booking information does not replace any written consent, release, identification verification or other documentation required before tattooing.

Reference Images & Intellectual Property

Clients should upload reference material they are permitted to share. A reference image does not guarantee an exact copy. Place of Art and its artists may decline requests that would improperly reproduce another artist's protected work or that the artist is not comfortable performing.

Photography & Portfolio Use

Photography for the studio's portfolio, website, social media or marketing should be based on the client's consent. Permission to use identifiable client imagery is separate from submitting a booking request.

Touch-Ups

Touch-up eligibility, timing and cost depend on the tattoo, healing, aftercare and artist. Touch-ups related to normal healing are evaluated by the original artist. Design changes, additional work, fading caused by improper aftercare or other damage are not automatically considered complimentary touch-ups.

Right to Refuse or Stop Service

Place of Art may decline, postpone or stop a service when reasonably necessary for safety, legal compliance, inability to obtain informed consent, abusive or threatening conduct, unrealistic project expectations or other legitimate business reasons permitted by law.

Studio & Online Booking Hours

Online appointment requests are offered Monday through Saturday between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM, subject to artist availability. Sunday is closed. Jessie-Ann Odell's online request window for small tattoos is 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Large tattoo projects with Jessie-Ann begin at 9:00 AM.
