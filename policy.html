Studio Rules

Studio Policy

Please review before submitting a tattoo request.

Professional studio-policy template only; have the final wording and digital consent workflow reviewed for your Oklahoma business before relying on it as legal advice.

Age & Identification

Tattoo services are for clients age 18 and older. Valid government-issued photo identification is required before tattooing.

Appointment Requests

An online submission is a request only. The appointment becomes confirmed only after Place of Art or the selected artist accepts it.

Deposits

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required for most appointments. Appointments priced at $500 or more require a $100 non-refundable deposit. Deposits are credited toward the tattoo when the appointment is completed as scheduled.

Rescheduling & No-Shows

Please give at least 48 hours notice to reschedule. Late cancellations, no-shows or repeated rescheduling may result in forfeiture of the deposit and a new deposit being required.

Pricing & Design Changes

Website prices may be starting prices. Final pricing can vary with size, placement, detail, complexity, color, shading and time. Significant design changes may change the price or appointment length.

Ability to Consent & Safety

The studio may decline or postpone service when a client cannot provide informed consent, appears impaired, or when tattooing would conflict with applicable health and safety requirements.

Reference Images

Reference images are used for direction and inspiration. Submission does not guarantee an exact copy. Artists may adapt composition, size and placement or decline work they are not comfortable performing.

Photography

Portfolio or marketing use of identifiable client images should be based on separate client permission.

Right to Refuse Service

Place of Art may decline, postpone or stop service for legitimate safety, legal, conduct or business reasons permitted by law.
