Privacy

Privacy Policy

How information submitted through the website is handled.

Information Collected

Booking requests may collect name, date of birth, email, phone number, tattoo details, placement, selected artist, requested time and reference images.

Use

Information is used to evaluate requests, communicate, plan tattoos, schedule appointments, process deposits and maintain appropriate studio records.

Security

Live client records should be stored in a secured backend with account controls. Full card information must not be stored in GitHub files and should be handled by a compliant payment provider.
