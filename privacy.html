Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Place of Art handles information submitted through the website.

Information We Collect

Appointment requests may collect your full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, tattoo description, placement, selected artist, requested date and time, and one or more reference images. Payment information should be processed directly by the connected payment provider rather than stored in public website files.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to evaluate tattoo requests, communicate with clients, plan designs, schedule appointments, process deposits, maintain appropriate studio records and provide tattoo services.

Reference Images

Reference images submitted for a tattoo request are used to evaluate and prepare for the requested project. They should be treated as private client-submitted material unless the client separately authorizes another use.

Access & Sharing

Booking information may be accessible to authorized Place of Art artists and staff who need it for scheduling or service delivery. Information may also be processed by hosting, database, communication and payment providers used to operate the website. Information may be disclosed when required by law or a regulatory authority.

Security

Live client records should be stored in a secured backend with account access controls. Full payment-card information should never be stored in this GitHub website and should instead be handled by a compliant payment provider such as Stripe or Square.

Retention

Client and procedure records should be retained for the period required by applicable law and studio recordkeeping obligations. Reference images and ordinary booking communications may be deleted when they are no longer reasonably needed, subject to recordkeeping requirements.
