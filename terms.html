Terms of Use

Terms for using the Place of Art website and online appointment request system.

Submitting a booking form or selecting a date and time does not create a confirmed appointment. Confirmation occurs only after Place of Art accepts the request.

Website Pricing

Prices shown online may be starting prices or category prices. A displayed starting price is not a guaranteed final quote unless the artist or studio specifically confirms it.

Payments

Deposits and other payments are governed by the Studio Policy and the payment provider's terms. Payment-card information should be collected only through the connected payment provider.

Art Gallery Sales

Original artwork may be one-of-a-kind. Availability is not guaranteed until a purchase or reservation is confirmed. Shipping, local pickup, taxes, returns and final sales terms should be displayed for each artwork when online sales are activated.

No Medical Advice

Website aftercare and general information is educational and does not replace individualized advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Clients should seek appropriate medical care for concerning symptoms or complications.

Changes

Place of Art may update website content, pricing, scheduling rules and studio policies from time to time. The version provided at the time of booking or appointment may apply to that service.
